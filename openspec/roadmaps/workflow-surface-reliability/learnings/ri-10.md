decisions:
- title: Land Obsidian ingress as one privacy-preserving canonical vertical
  outcome: Vaults are selected by a 20-hex opaque HMAC key with a config-version fingerprint;
    paths, folders, and raw configured-source snapshots never enter durable payloads,
    discovery, results, logs, or alerts, and readiness fails closed without a path.
- title: Separate retained failures from new failures
  outcome: A note whose bounded retry budget is spent and whose file has not changed is
    reported as skipped with a retained diagnostic projected as a warning, so a single
    permanently invalid note can no longer fail every later scan or re-alert on every poll.
- title: Compare durable claims against canonical identities, not raw rows
  outcome: Two notes clipping one page stay two rows under one canonical primary; the PR-tier
    delta helper and the failure classifier compare claims against a primary-identity delta,
    which is identical to the row delta for every other source.
